What this is

Every year I make a little page where you can get a rough idea of what kind of person I am. This page isn't finished. But it never will be. I'll be thinking about it, changing it and editing it all through 2026. And a year from now I'll make the next one, about 2027 — and I'll keep changing, filling in and editing that one too.

In short

My name is Zhenia [ˈʐɛ.nʲə]. I grew up in Russia, lived in Bishkek from March 2022, and since February 2025 I live in Berlin. I'm a designer, editor, entrepreneur and a consultant in the SFBT approach. Right now I'm actively trying to bring AI into my life.

What matters to me

I'm trying to put into words a few of the main things I go by in life. So far, like this:

Brain
I'm interested in everything that affects how we feel, think and behave.
Design
I care that it works, and that it's clear and beautiful.
Fun
I care that there's fun in it. I'm drawn to the new and the unusual.
Freedom
I care about feeling free.

Expertise and interest

In some topics I have expertise, in others — only interest. But I enjoy talking and thinking about all of it: design, information design, interfaces, starting and running channels, applying AI in different fields, urbanism, SFBT, narrative practice, contemporary power, psychotherapy, coaching, stand-up, impact stand-up, the ethics of humour, making projects, pet projects, bots, life and integration in Germany, contemporary German culture, group psychotherapy and coaching, cultural differences, friendship and communities, social games, board games, video games, educational ones, making games, linguistics, foreign and constructed languages, anything unusual. To be continued.

I used to work at Tinkoff Journal, JetBrains and Huntflow. I didn't enjoy working at big companies.
