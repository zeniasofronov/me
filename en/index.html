What this is

Every year I make a little page where you can get a rough idea of what kind of person I am. This page isn't finished. But it never will be. I'll be thinking about it, changing it and editing it all through 2026. And a year from now I'll make the next one, about 2027 — and I'll keep changing, filling in and editing that one too.

In short

My name is Ženja [ˈʐɛ.nʲə]. I grew up in Russia, lived in Bishkek from March 2022, and since February 2025 I live in Berlin. I'm a designer, editor, entrepreneur and a consultant in the SFBT approach. Right now I'm actively trying to bring AI into my life.

What matters to me

I'm trying to put into words a few of the main things I go by in life. So far, like this:

The world and people
I'm interested in everything that affects how we feel, think and behave.
Design and projects
I care that it works, and that it's clear and beautiful.
Fun and freedom
I care that there's fun in it, and about feeling free. I'm drawn to the new and the unusual.

Expertise and interest

Information design, artificial intelligence, SFBT, impact stand-up, culture, games, languages and everything unusual.

What I did before

I used to work at Tinkoff Journal, JetBrains and Huntflow. I didn't enjoy working at big companies. Mostly I did my own projects and worked as a freelance designer and consultant.

For example, I made «Велосипедизацию Санкт-Петербурга» (Cycling St. Petersburg), «Все секонд-хенды Санкт-Петербурга и Москвы» (Every Second-Hand Shop in St. Petersburg and Moscow) and «Сосединг» (Neighbouring). The links to the sites themselves are all dead now :–(
