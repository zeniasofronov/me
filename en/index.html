What this is

Every year I make a little page where you can get a rough idea of what kind of person I am. This page isn't finished. But it never will be. I'll be thinking about it, changing it and editing it all through 2026. And a year from now I'll make the next one, about 2027 — and I'll keep changing, filling in and editing that one too.

Who I am

My name is Ženja [ˈʐɛ.nʲə]. I grew up in Russia, lived in Bishkek from March 2022, and since February 2025 I live in Berlin. I'm a designer, editor, entrepreneur and a consultant in the SFBT approach. Right now I'm actively trying to bring AI into my life.

What matters to me

I'm trying to put into words a few of the main things I go by in life. So far, like this:

  • The world and people. I'm interested in everything that affects how we feel, think and behave.
  • Design and projects. I care that it works, and that it's clear and beautiful.
  • Fun and freedom. I care that there's fun in it, and about feeling free. I'm drawn to the new and the unusual.

What I do now

Something interesting and useful:

  • Луч внимания (Ray of Attention). My personal channel. I write about whatever I'm into right now. In Russian.
  • Чтобы не выгорать (So You Don't Burn Out). My biggest channel. I write about how to work, rest and cope. In Russian.
  • Wie man klarkommt (How to Cope). A local Berlin mutation of the "Чтобы не выгорать" ("So You Don't Burn Out") channel. I write about how to cope in a new country. In German.
  • Жизнь и ИИ (Life and AI). A channel where I try to make sense of AI. I write about life in a world with AI, before it wipes us out. In Russian.

Something fun and cringe:

  • Twitter. My personal Twitter. I post jokes I like, for 2 likes. In Russian, sometimes in German.
  • Instagram. Stories about life in Berlin. I post observations and trade reels. In Russian, in meme, and sometimes in German.

  • Mostly I did my own projects. For example, I made «Велосипедизацию Санкт-Петербурга» (Cycling St. Petersburg), «Все секонд-хенды Санкт-Петербурга и Москвы» (Every Second-Hand Shop in St. Petersburg and Moscow) and «Сосединг» (Neighbouring). The links to the sites themselves are all dead now :–(
  • And I worked as a freelance designer. In my work I meticulously blend rigorous information design with wild fun. I uploaded a few pictures from the past to Google Drive.
