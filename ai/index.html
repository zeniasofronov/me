What this kind of page is for

This is a personal «AI context» file: a single document that gives a model enough grounded detail to reason about a specific person. Files like this are pasted into an assistant to:

  • get briefed before a meeting or an intro call;
  • draft a message to the person in a tone that will land;
  • judge whether a project, role, or another person is a good fit;
  • decide how to communicate — directness, humour, language, formality;
  • answer factual questions without the asker having to dig through a website;
  • simulate the person's likely point of view on something.

It is deliberately explicit and over-complete, because a model cannot read between the lines or ask follow-up questions the way a person in a live conversation can.

Working style

  • Editorial systems and content operations; launching or running a channel or publication.
  • Hands-on, pragmatic integration of AI into a product or a team's workflow.
  • Solution-focused coaching and group facilitation; narrative practice.

Poor fit

  • Large corporations, heavy process, low autonomy — he has tried this and disliked it.
  • Environments that require polished, cautious, jargon-heavy communication.
  • Work that must stay entirely serious, with no room for fun.
  • Anything that needs fluent German right now, or an English-only setting where fine nuance matters — workable, but not his strength.
  • Pro-Kremlin or «United Russia»-aligned organisations — see positions below.

On process: his favourite word when making things is «наговнякать» — roughly «to slap something together roughly». Read this as comfort with quick drafts and MVPs; he does not expect things to arrive polished, and prefers to iterate.
